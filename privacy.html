Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What we access

With the channel owner's authorization, Allen Analytics reads, in read-only mode, YouTube Analytics and YouTube channel data (such as views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, audience geography and age, and video titles) through the YouTube Analytics, YouTube Data and YouTube Reporting APIs. It cannot upload, edit or delete any content.

How we use it

The data is used only to produce weekly performance reports for the channel owner and their content team.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer the data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Reports and the authorization token are stored privately and are accessible only to the channel's content team. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can ask us to delete stored data by email.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
